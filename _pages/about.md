# Welcome

I am Md Abu Shyeed, a PhD Candidate in Analytical Chemistry at Kyung Hee University, South Korea.
My research focuses on single molecule detection, biosensors, super-resolution imaging, and nanomaterials.
I am interested in developing advanced analytical platforms for biomedical applications and molecular-level sensing.
