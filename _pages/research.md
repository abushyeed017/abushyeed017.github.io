# Research Overview

My research focuses on developing advanced analytical platforms for studying biological systems at the molecular level. By integrating nanomaterials, super-resolution imaging, and artificial intelligence, my goal is to develop highly sensitive tools for real-time visualization and sensing of biological processes.

# Current Research Direction

## Real-Time CRISPR-Based Molecular Tracking in Living Systems

My current research interest focuses on developing real-time CRISPR tracking platforms for visualizing molecular events inside living systems.

This project aims to combine CRISPR-based molecular recognition with advanced imaging and analytical techniques to achieve dynamic monitoring of biological processes at the molecular scale.

Research objectives include:

- Real-time visualization of target nucleic acid dynamics
- Development of sensitive molecular tracking strategies
- Integration of biosensing and advanced microscopy approaches
- Understanding biological processes through direct molecular observation

# Previous Research

## Deep Learning-Assisted Light-Sheet Superresolution Sensing of Six-Dimensional Nanoparticle Dynamics in Living Cells

**ACS Sensors (2026)**

My recent research developed a deep learning-assisted light-sheet superresolution (DL–LSSR) sensing framework for direct extraction of multidimensional nanoparticle dynamics from optical signals.

The developed platform integrates light-sheet superresolution sensing with deep learning to reconstruct six-dimensional nanoparticle states:

- Three-dimensional position (x, y, z)
- Rotational orientation (θ, φ)
- Instantaneous velocity (v)

The framework enables automated tracking and classification of nanoparticle transport behaviors in living cells.

Using fucoidan-conjugated gold nanorods as a biological model, this work investigated nanoparticle-cell interactions and revealed uptake-associated orientation behavior during cellular entry.

A major advantage of this approach was the significant improvement in analytical speed, achieving approximately 99-fold faster processing compared with conventional analysis workflows.
